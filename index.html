How We Work

Customer On-Boarding Process

A transparent 12-step journey from first meeting to final handover — so you always know what comes next.

01

Understanding Customer Requirement

  • First meeting with the client
  • Discuss needs and expectations
  • Understand budget and timeline
  • Confirm project timeline
  • Note lifestyle, preferences, and space usage
02

Concept & Theme Discussion

  • Show different interior design themes
  • Understand client's style and preferences
  • Discuss colors, materials, and finishes
  • Suggest the best-suited design concept
  • Align design with budget and lifestyle
03

Site Visit & Measurement

  • Designer visits the site
  • Take accurate measurements
  • Check site condition
  • Capture photo and video for reference
  • Collect all technical details for design planning
04

Concept Drafting & Designing

  • Prepare the actual design concept
  • Create layout and design drawings
  • Finalize theme, colors, and materials
  • Share design ideas with the client
  • Make changes based on client feedback
05

BOQ / BOM — Rough Estimation

  • Prepare BOQ / BOM (list of materials)
  • Estimate approximate project cost
  • Explain cost breakup to the client
  • Adjust scope as per budget if required
  • Get approval to move ahead
06

Final Quote

  • Prepare final BOQ and quotation
  • Confirm final design, materials, and pricing
  • Share complete cost details
  • Get client's final approval
  • Proceed to execution stage
07

2D Drawing Making

  • Prepare detailed 2D drawings
  • Layout planning and elevations
  • Furniture and cabinet details
  • Finalize material placements
  • Use drawings for execution clarity
08

3D Drawing Making

  • Create 3D views based on approved 2D drawings
  • Show actual colors, materials, and finishes
  • Help client visualize the final look
  • Make revisions if required
  • Get final design approval
09

Material Procurement @ Market

  • Visit market with the client
  • Select materials, finishes, and fittings
  • Finalize brands and specifications
  • Procure approved materials
  • Ensure quality and budget control
10

Work Execution

  • Start actual on-site work
  • Follow approved drawings and designs
  • Work under expert supervision
  • Regular quality checks
  • Timely progress updates to client
11

Photoshoot

  • Professional photoshoot after completion
  • Capture final interiors and details
  • Use images for records and social media
  • Share selected photos with the client
12

Handover 🎉

  • Final site inspection with the client
  • Handover of completed project
  • Keys handed over to the client
  • Small handover celebration
  • Project successfully closed

About Us

Who We Are

The One Furniture is a modern furniture and interior solutions brand based in Vasai West, Mumbai. With a rich legacy and industry experience dating back to 2005 that spans from Dubai to Mumbai, we have quickly established ourselves as a trusted destination for high-quality, stylish, and functional furniture solutions for both residential and commercial spaces.

The brand operates under Ramjisheth Furniture Private Limited, a growing furniture manufacturing and trading company incorporated in 2021. Built on the idea of delivering complete furniture and interior solutions under one roof — from living room sofas and bedroom wardrobes to office furniture and customized décor solutions.

Founded with a vision to simplify furniture buying and interior design, The One Furniture focuses on a direct-to-customer approach, eliminating unnecessary middlemen and making quality furniture more accessible and affordable.

Since its inception, the company has grown steadily by leveraging local craftsmanship, modern production techniques, and a customer-first mindset. With a dedicated team and a growing client base across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, and surrounding regions, the brand continues to expand its reach while maintaining high standards of quality and service.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project.

Our projects span a wide range depending on scope and finishes:

  • Under ₹5,00,000 — Basic fit-out & soft furnishing
  • ₹5,00,000 – ₹10,00,000 — Standard modular design
  • ₹10,00,000 – ₹25,00,000 — Full residential design
  • ₹25,00,000 – ₹50,00,000 — Premium design with custom furniture
  • ₹50,00,000 – ₹1,00,00,000+ — Luxury turnkey projects

We work with every budget and always recommend a consultation first so we can tailor the proposal to your needs.

We are fluent in all major interior design styles including:

  • Modern & Contemporary — clean lines, neutral palettes
  • Minimalist — less is more, decluttered spaces
  • Scandinavian — warm woods, functional elegance
  • Mid-Century Modern — retro with organic forms
  • Industrial — raw materials, exposed elements
  • Traditional & Classic — rich details, timeless appeal
  • Eclectic & Transitional — a curated mix of styles

During consultation, we help you identify your preferred style through mood boards and visual references.

We handle all residential configurations:

  • 1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 4 BHK apartments
  • Villas & independent houses
  • Duplexes & penthouses
  • Commercial & office spaces
  • Hospitality & F&B outlets

Whether it is a compact studio or a sprawling villa, our team designs for every space optimally.

Timelines vary by project size and complexity, but general estimates are:

  • 1 BHK / 2 BHK — 6 to 10 weeks
  • 3 BHK / 4 BHK — 10 to 16 weeks
  • Villa / Large Residence — 16 to 28 weeks
  • Commercial Turnkey — Depends on area and scope

We share a detailed project schedule after the initial consultation and concept approval.

We design every area of your home or commercial space, including:

  • Living Room — sofa, TV unit, false ceiling, lighting, partitions
  • Dining Room — table, chairs, bar unit, wallpaper & paint
  • Kitchen — modular layouts, appliances, countertops, storage
  • Master & other Bedrooms — bed, wardrobes, study, dressing
  • Bathrooms — vanity, fixtures, tiles, accessories
  • Home Office, Pooja Room, Kids' Room, Balcony & more

We work at any stage of your property journey:

  • Bare Shell — we design from scratch alongside construction
  • Under Construction — we plan ahead so execution is seamless on possession
  • Ready to Move In — we begin immediately with full fit-out
  • Existing Home / Renovation — we assess the space and redesign with minimal disruption

We source from premium vendors across all material categories:

  • Flooring — Marble, Tiles, Engineered Wood, Vinyl, Carpet
  • Walls — Wallpaper, Textured Paint, Paneling, Stone cladding
  • Furniture — Custom-made, modular, and curated from top brands
  • Lighting — Recessed, pendant, cove, statement & smart lighting

All materials are selected to match your chosen design style and budget.

Getting started is simple — just 3 steps:

  • 1. Fill the enquiry form — share your name, location, flat config, possession status & scope of work
  • 2. Free consultation — our designer meets you (in person or virtually) to understand your vision
  • 3. Proposal & kickoff — we present concept designs, get your approval, and begin execution
