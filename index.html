ABOUT US

Who We Are

Established in 2005, The One Furniture has become a trusted name in luxury interior design and custom furniture solutions across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai. With nearly two decades of experience, we are passionate about transforming spaces into elegant, functional, and inspiring environments that reflect modern lifestyles and personal taste.

At The One Furniture, we believe that great interiors are not just about decoration — they are about creating experiences, comfort, and timeless beauty. Every home, office, showroom, or commercial space carries a unique identity, and our goal is to design interiors that perfectly match the vision, personality, and lifestyle of our clients.

Over the years, we have successfully completed a wide range of residential and commercial interior projects, earning the trust of homeowners, business owners, architects, and corporate clients through our commitment to quality, creativity, and customer satisfaction. From luxurious living rooms and modern modular kitchens to elegant office interiors and customized furniture solutions, we focus on delivering spaces that combine aesthetics with functionality.

Our journey started with a simple vision — to provide premium-quality interiors and furniture solutions with professional service, innovative designs, and exceptional craftsmanship. Today, The One Furniture continues to grow as a reliable interior brand known for attention to detail, modern concepts, and personalized design experiences.

Based in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai, our team of experienced designers, project managers, skilled craftsmen, and consultants work together to deliver complete turnkey interior solutions. We carefully understand each client’s requirements before planning and executing designs that suit their space, budget, and expectations. Every project is treated with dedication, precision, and creativity to ensure outstanding results.

What makes The One Furniture different is our strong focus on quality craftsmanship and personalized service. We use premium materials, modern finishing techniques, and innovative design concepts to create interiors that are stylish, durable, and practical for everyday living. Our team constantly stays updated with the latest interior trends and modern architecture styles to bring fresh and creative ideas into every project.

Customer satisfaction has always been the foundation of our success. We believe in building long-term relationships through honesty, professionalism, transparent communication, and timely project execution. From the first consultation to the final handover, we ensure a smooth and stress-free experience for every client.

At The One Furniture, we understand that interiors are an important investment for every family and business. That is why we focus on delivering not only beautiful spaces but also comfort, functionality, and long-term value. Every project we create reflects our passion for design excellence and our dedication to superior workmanship.

As we continue our journey, our mission remains the same — to design inspiring interiors that elevate lifestyles and create memorable spaces filled with elegance, comfort, and sophistication.

With years of trust, creativity, and expertise, The One Furniture proudly continues to serve clients across Mumbai and Navi Mumbai, turning ideas into extraordinary interior experiences since 2005.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your project.

Our projects span a wide range depending on scope and finishes:

  • Under ₹5,00,000 — Basic fit-out & soft furnishing
  • ₹5,00,000 – ₹10,00,000 — Standard modular design
  • ₹10,00,000 – ₹25,00,000 — Full residential design
  • ₹25,00,000 – ₹50,00,000 — Premium design with custom furniture
  • ₹50,00,000 – ₹1,00,00,000+ — Luxury turnkey projects

We work with every budget and always recommend a consultation first so we can tailor the proposal to your needs.

We are fluent in all major interior design styles including:

  • Modern & Contemporary — clean lines, neutral palettes
  • Minimalist — less is more, decluttered spaces
  • Scandinavian — warm woods, functional elegance
  • Mid-Century Modern — retro with organic forms
  • Industrial — raw materials, exposed elements
  • Traditional & Classic — rich details, timeless appeal
  • Eclectic & Transitional — a curated mix of styles

During consultation, we help you identify your preferred style through mood boards and visual references.

We handle all residential configurations:

  • 1 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK, 4 BHK apartments
  • Villas & independent houses
  • Duplexes & penthouses
  • Commercial & office spaces
  • Hospitality & F&B outlets

Whether it is a compact studio or a sprawling villa, our team designs for every space optimally.

Timelines vary by project size and complexity, but general estimates are:

  • 1 BHK / 2 BHK — 6 to 10 weeks
  • 3 BHK / 4 BHK — 10 to 16 weeks
  • Villa / Large Residence — 16 to 28 weeks
  • Commercial Turnkey — Depends on area and scope

We share a detailed project schedule after the initial consultation and concept approval.

We design every area of your home or commercial space, including:

  • Living Room — sofa, TV unit, false ceiling, lighting, partitions
  • Dining Room — table, chairs, bar unit, wallpaper & paint
  • Kitchen — modular layouts, appliances, countertops, storage
  • Master & other Bedrooms — bed, wardrobes, study, dressing
  • Bathrooms — vanity, fixtures, tiles, accessories
  • Home Office, Pooja Room, Kids' Room, Balcony & more

We work at any stage of your property journey:

  • Bare Shell — we design from scratch alongside construction
  • Under Construction — we plan ahead so execution is seamless on possession
  • Ready to Move In — we begin immediately with full fit-out
  • Existing Home / Renovation — we assess the space and redesign with minimal disruption

We source from premium vendors across all material categories:

  • Flooring — Marble, Tiles, Engineered Wood, Vinyl, Carpet
  • Walls — Wallpaper, Textured Paint, Paneling, Stone cladding
  • Furniture — Custom-made, modular, and curated from top brands
  • Lighting — Recessed, pendant, cove, statement & smart lighting

All materials are selected to match your chosen design style and budget.

Getting started is simple — just 3 steps:

  • 1. Fill the enquiry form — share your name, location, flat config, possession status & scope of work
  • 2. Free consultation — our designer meets you (in person or virtually) to understand your vision
  • 3. Proposal & kickoff — we present concept designs, get your approval, and begin execution
